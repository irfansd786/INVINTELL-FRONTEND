==================================================
INVINTELL LANDING PAGE PLATFORM VIDEO FOLDER
==================================================

Place your product demonstration video file inside this directory:
frontend/public/video/

Recommended video naming:
- demo.mp4  (Default auto-loaded video)
- demo.webm
- video.mp4

When you place your video file here (e.g., frontend/public/video/demo.mp4), 
the INVINTELL Landing Page Platform Preview component will automatically 
detect and play your video in high-definition with controls!
